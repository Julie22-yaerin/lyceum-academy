# IGCSE Additional Mathematics — Cambridge (CIE 0606)

Bridges IGCSE Maths (Extended) and AS-Level. Introduces calculus, logarithms, and higher algebra.

## Official syllabus
- [Cambridge IGCSE Additional Mathematics 0606 — subject page](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/programmes-and-qualifications/cambridge-igcse-additional-mathematics-0606/)

## Assessment
- Paper 1 & Paper 2, each 2h, 80 marks, 50% (calculator allowed). No coursework.

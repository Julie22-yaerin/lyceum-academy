## Edexcel International GCSE — quick codes
- Mathematics A — **4MA1** · Further Pure Mathematics — **4PM1**
- Physics — **4PH1** · Chemistry — **4CH1** · Biology — **4BI1**
- Official specs: [Pearson International GCSE](https://qualifications.pearson.com/en/qualifications/edexcel-international-gcses.html)

## Notes
- CIE Sciences use **Paper 6 (Alternative to Practical)** in written series.
- Maths 0580 = Core (C–G) or Extended (A*–C) tiers.
- Codes verified 2026-07-19 for the 2025–2027 (Maths) / 2026–2028 (Sciences) cycles.

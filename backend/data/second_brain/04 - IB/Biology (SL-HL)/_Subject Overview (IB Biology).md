# IB Biology (SL/HL) — first exams 2025

Concept-based guide organised around four themes, each explored at four levels of organisation (molecules, cells, organisms, ecosystems). No options, no Paper 3.

## Official guide
- [IBO — Biology in the DP](https://www.ibo.org/university-admission/latest-curriculum-updates/biology-updates/)

## Assessment
- Paper 1 (1A MCQ + 1B data-based), Paper 2. HL = extra depth. **Scientific Investigation** IA = 20%.

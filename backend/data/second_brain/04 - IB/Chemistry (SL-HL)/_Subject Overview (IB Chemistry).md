# IB Chemistry (SL/HL) — first exams 2025

Built on two organising concepts: **Structure** (what things are made of) and **Reactivity** (how/why they change). Structure determines reactivity, which transforms structure. No options, no Paper 3.

## Official guide
- [IBO — Chemistry in the DP](https://ibo.org/programmes/diploma-programme/curriculum/sciences/chemistry/)

## Assessment
- Paper 1 (1A MCQ + 1B data-based), Paper 2. HL = extra depth. **Scientific Investigation** IA = 20%.

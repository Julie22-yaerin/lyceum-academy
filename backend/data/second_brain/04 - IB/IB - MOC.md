## Notes
- Math (AA & AI): 5 common topics — Number & Algebra, Functions, Geometry & Trigonometry, Statistics & Probability, Calculus. HL adds depth + the AA/AI "toolkit".
- Sciences assessment: Paper 1 (1A MCQ + 1B data-based), Paper 2, plus the Internal Assessment (Scientific Investigation, 20%).
- Verify each guide on [ibo.org](https://www.ibo.org/) each cycle.

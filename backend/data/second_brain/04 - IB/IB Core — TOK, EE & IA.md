# IB Core — TOK, EE & Internal Assessment

## Theory of Knowledge (TOK)
- Explores how we know what we know; assessed via TOK Exhibition + 1600-word essay.
- [IBO — TOK](https://www.ibo.org/programmes/diploma-programme/curriculum/theory-of-knowledge/)

## Extended Essay (EE)
- 4000-word independent research essay in a chosen subject.
- [IBO — Extended Essay](https://www.ibo.org/programmes/diploma-programme/curriculum/extended-essay/)

## Internal Assessment (IA) — Sciences & Maths
- **Sciences**: the Scientific Investigation (individual report, 20% of grade).
- **Mathematics**: the Exploration (individual written report, 20%).

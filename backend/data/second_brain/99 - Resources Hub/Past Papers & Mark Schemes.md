# Past Papers & Mark Schemes — Master List

## Cambridge (CIE) — IGCSE & A-Level
- [GCE Guide — Past Papers](https://papers.gceguide.com/) — clean CIE archive, QP + MS + examiner reports.
- [PapaCambridge — Past Papers](https://pastpapers.papacambridge.com/) — large CIE/CAIE archive.
- [Dynamic Papers](https://dynamicpapers.com/) — CIE + Edexcel, topical filtering.
- [Cambridge International — official past paper info](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/support-and-training-for-schools/past-papers/) — authoritative, recent-year access via school login.

## Edexcel (Pearson) — International GCSE & A-Level
- [Pearson — Past Papers & Mark Schemes](https://qualifications.pearson.com/en/support/support-topics/exams/past-papers.html) — official.
- [Physics & Maths Tutor — Edexcel IGCSE](https://www.physicsandmathstutor.com/) — topical + full papers.

## AQA — GCSE
- [AQA — Find Past Papers and Mark Schemes](https://www.aqa.org.uk/find-past-papers-and-mark-schemes) — official.
- [Physics & Maths Tutor — AQA GCSE](https://www.physicsandmathstutor.com/) — topic questions + solutions.
- [Maths Genie](https://www.mathsgenie.co.uk/) — GCSE maths papers by grade.

## Topical / question-bank practice
- [Save My Exams — Topic Questions](https://www.savemyexams.com/) — board-tagged questions + model answers (subscription for full).
- [Physics & Maths Tutor — Topic Questions](https://www.physicsandmathstutor.com/) — free topical PDFs.

> Always pair a past paper with its **mark scheme** and, where available, the **examiner report** to learn command-word expectations.

# Curated Revision Sites & Channels

## All-subject platforms
- [Save My Exams](https://www.savemyexams.com/) — board-specific revision notes, topic questions, flashcards (IGCSE/GCSE/A-Level).
- [Physics & Maths Tutor (PMT)](https://www.physicsandmathstutor.com/) — free notes, topic questions, past papers for Maths & Sciences.
- [ZNotes](https://znotes.org/) — concise community CIE/Edexcel summary notes.
- [Khan Academy](https://www.khanacademy.org/) — conceptual foundations, especially Maths & Physics.

## Mathematics
- [Corbettmaths](https://corbettmaths.com/) — videos, worksheets, 5-a-day (GCSE/IGCSE).
- [Maths Genie](https://www.mathsgenie.co.uk/) — graded papers + video solutions.
- [Dr Frost Maths](https://www.drfrostmaths.com/) — structured practice by objective.
- [ExamSolutions](https://www.examsolutions.net/) — A-Level maths worked examples.

## Sciences (video)
- [Cognito](https://www.youtube.com/@cognitoedu) — GCSE/IGCSE Bio/Chem/Physics.
- [FreeScienceLessons](https://www.youtube.com/@Freesciencelessons) — GCSE science.
- [Cambridge in 5 Minutes / SnapRevise style channels] — A-Level topic recaps.
- [Physics Online](https://www.youtube.com/@PhysicsOnline) — GCSE/A-Level physics.

## A-Level specific
- [ExamSolutions](https://www.examsolutions.net/) — Maths & Further Maths.
- [Chemguide](https://www.chemguide.co.uk/) — deep A-Level chemistry explanations.
- [A-Level Biology (Bioknowledgy / Snab)] — cross-check with CIE 9700 spec.

> Curated for reliability. Third-party notes are revision aids — the **exam-board spec** remains the source of truth.
